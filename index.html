Two apps. Use either one, or both.

Turn visitors into customers, then keep the conversation going.

Funnels builds and publishes the pages that capture leads and sales. Conversations gives your team one inbox for live chat, SMS and email. They are separate products with separate sign-ups, so adopt one without the other.

Funnels

Design multi-step landing pages with a block editor, publish them on your own domain, and see what converts.

  • Block-based page builder with version history and undo
  • Opt-in forms, offers, order bumps and one-click upsells
  • Custom domains with DNS verification and safe fallbacks
  • Per-step analytics: visits, opt-ins, orders, earnings per view
  • Templates for lead magnets, webinars, launches and more
  • Signed webhooks for every lead and sale

Conversations

One shared inbox for live chat, SMS and email, with assignment, routing and a chat widget for your site.

  • Live chat widget you embed with a single script tag
  • SMS and email threads per contact, with opt-out handling
  • Inboxes, round-robin assignment, snooze and priority
  • Realtime delivery, read receipts and presence
  • Quick replies with merge fields, internal notes, @mentions
  • Office hours, away messages and OTP verification

AI where it saves time

In Funnels

Generate a whole funnel from a short brief, rewrite copy in a different tone, get headline variants, and read plain-language insights about your weakest step.

In Conversations

Suggested replies, thread summaries, triage that flags urgent or spam messages, and an optional auto-responder that hands off to a person when unsure.

AI is optional. Every feature has an offline fallback, and nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you configure a key.
